# Real Estate

Agents, teams, and brokerages. A high-trust, high-value transaction, the site sells **the agent's expertise, local authority, and track record**, and captures leads (buyers and sellers).

## Brand voice & tone

- **Professional, confident, locally expert.** "Your {city} real estate expert", authority on the specific market.
- **Personal brand-forward.** Real estate is relationship-driven; the agent's face, name, and story matter more than the brokerage logo.
- Clean, polished, trustworthy aesthetic, quality photography of homes and of the agent. Avoid cheap/cluttered "listing portal" looks.

## Target keywords

- Role + place: `real estate agent {city}`, `realtor {neighborhood}`, `{city} homes for sale`, `sell my house {city}`
- Intent: `home valuation {city}`, `what's my home worth`, `first-time home buyer {city}`, `luxury homes {city}`
- Authority: `best realtor {city}`, `{neighborhood} real estate market`

## Page set & structure

**Home · Listings/Featured properties · Buy · Sell · About the agent · Neighborhoods/Market · Reviews · Contact.**

Homepage order:
1. Hero, agent or signature property, value prop, dual CTA: **Find a home / What's my home worth**
2. Quick search or featured listings
3. Buy / Sell paths, two clear journeys
4. About the agent, photo, story, credentials, local roots
5. Track record, homes sold, days-on-market, sale-to-list ratio, awards
6. Reviews / client stories (deeply persuasive)
7. Home-valuation lead capture + contact + areas served

## Recommended library combo

- **Brand Kit:** `enterprise-blue` (default, polished, trustworthy). Use `consulting-navy` for a corporate brokerage feel or `minimal-consultancy` for a clean modern personal brand.
- **Prompt:** `real-estate-agent` (Professional Services).
- **Template:** none yet, build from structure. (Backlog: a real-estate/agent template.)

## Conversion patterns

- **Two lead magnets:** "Search homes" (buyers) and "Free home valuation" (sellers), capture both intents.
- **Track record numbers** (homes sold, avg. days on market) convert better than adjectives.
- Client reviews with names/photos and an authentic agent bio drive contact.
- Neighborhood/market content builds local-authority SEO and traps long-tail searches.

## Compliance & credibility notes

- **Fair-housing compliance:** never reference race, religion, family status, national origin, etc. in copy or imagery framing ("perfect for a Christian family" = violation). Keep descriptions about the property, not the buyer.
- Display license number and brokerage per local/state rules; include the Equal Housing Opportunity logo where required.
- Don't misstate sold figures or guarantee sale prices/timelines.
- Structure/tone guidance only, not legal or real-estate-licensing advice; recommend a compliance review of fair-housing language.
