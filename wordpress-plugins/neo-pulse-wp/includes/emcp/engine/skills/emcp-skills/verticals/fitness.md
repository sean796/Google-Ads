# Fitness & Studios

Two energies live here: **high-energy gym/CrossFit/bootcamp** (bold, motivating, results-driven) and **calm boutique studio/yoga/pilates** (mindful, restorative). Pick the lane from the brief and commit the whole design to it.

## Brand voice & tone

- **Gym/CrossFit lane:** bold, motivating, second-person ("crush your goals", "your first class is on us"). High-contrast, energetic photography, strong type.
- **Yoga/pilates lane:** mindful, grounded, welcoming to beginners ("all levels", "find your flow"). Soft palette, calm imagery, airy layout.
- Either way: **community-forward**: fitness sells belonging as much as workouts. Show real members/classes.

## Target keywords

- Type + place: `gym {city}`, `yoga studio {city}`, `personal trainer {city}`, `CrossFit {city}`, `pilates near me`, `spin class {city}`
- Offer: `free trial class`, `first week free`, `no-commitment membership`, `beginner yoga`, `small group training`
- Goal: `weight loss program`, `strength training`, `prenatal yoga`, `mobility`

## Page set & structure

**Home · Classes/Programs · Schedule · Membership/Pricing · Trainers/Instructors · Free Trial.**

Homepage order:
1. Hero, motion/energy (or calm) imagery, value prop, **Free trial / First class free** CTA
2. Social proof band, member count, transformations or community vibe, review stars
3. Classes/programs grid, what's offered, intensity/level
4. Schedule preview / link to full timetable
5. Meet the trainers/instructors, credentials + personality
6. Membership pricing, clear tiers, "no contract" if true
7. Trial offer + location/hours + CTA

## Recommended library combo

- **Brand Kit:** `fitness-energy` (default, high-energy gym). Use `bold-studio` for a strong boutique/branded-studio look, or `holistic-earth` for yoga/pilates/wellness studios.
- **Prompts:** `fitness-club` (primary, gyms, training, high-energy) and `yoga-studio` (calm/boutique alternate). Match the prompt to the lane.
- **Template:** none yet, build from structure.

## Conversion patterns

- **Free trial / first-class-free** is the dominant CTA, remove price friction to get them in the door.
- **Class schedule** must be easy to find and current, it's the most-visited page.
- Transformations/community photos and member testimonials drive signups.
- Transparent, no-contract membership pricing reduces hesitation.

## Compliance & credibility notes

- **No guaranteed results** ("lose 20 lbs in 30 days"). Use "designed to help you…".
- Health-screening / par-Q forms and waivers: note them; recommend consulting a physician before starting.
- Trainer certifications lend credibility, surface them.
- Structure/tone guidance only, not medical or fitness-prescription advice.
