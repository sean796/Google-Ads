# Food & Restaurant

The broadest vertical, spans bakeries, cafés, pizzerias, sushi bars, taco shops, juice bars, ice-cream shops, and more. **Route to the sub-type first** (the prompt category carries one prompt per cuisine), then apply the structure below. The site sells appetite and atmosphere, and removes friction to a visit or an order.

## Brand voice & tone

- **Appetite-first and atmospheric.** Big, beautiful food photography is non-negotiable, it's the product.
- Match the establishment's energy: fine dining = refined/restrained; café/bakery = warm/cozy; taco/pizza = lively/bold; wine bar = moody/sophisticated.
- Sensory, evocative copy ("wood-fired", "small-batch", "made fresh daily"). Keep it short, the photos do the talking.

## Target keywords

- Type + place: `{cuisine} restaurant {city}` (e.g. `pizza {city}`, `sushi near me`), `best bakery {city}`, `coffee shop {neighborhood}`, `brunch {city}`
- Action: `order online`, `book a table {city}`, `{cuisine} delivery`, `catering {city}`, `private dining`
- Discovery: `dog-friendly café`, `gluten-free bakery {city}`, `happy hour {city}`

## Page set & structure

**Home · Menu · About/Story · Gallery · Reservations/Order · Location & Hours.** (Contact/hours/map must be effortless to find, it's the #1 reason people visit a restaurant site.)

Homepage order:
1. Hero, hero dish or interior shot, name, **Reserve / Order / View menu** CTA
2. Hours + location band high up (often the whole reason for the visit)
3. Featured menu items or categories (with photos)
4. Story / atmosphere, what makes it special
5. Gallery, food + space + people
6. Reviews / press mentions
7. Reservation/order CTA + map + hours + social

## Recommended library combo

- **Brand Kit:** `restaurant-warm` (default, versatile, inviting). Use `fine-dining` for upscale, `cafe-warm` for cafés/bakeries, `wine-bar` for bars/lounges.
- **Prompts:** whole `food-dining` category (`prompt_slugs: []`), pick the closest cuisine prompt at build time (`pizza-restaurant`, `bakery`, `coffee-shop`, `sushi-bar`, `taco-shop`, `juice-bar`, `ice-cream-shop`, `outdoor-cafe`). This is the reference case for the empty-array "use whole category" convention.
- **Templates:** `golden-crumb-bakery` (bakery/café) and `aroma-more-than-coffee` (coffee shop) are real starting points; for other cuisines, build from structure.

## Conversion patterns

- **Hours + location + "Order/Reserve"** above the fold, most restaurant-site visits are utility ("are they open? where? can I book?").
- Menu must be **real HTML/text, not a PDF**: PDFs kill mobile and SEO.
- Online-order / reservation integrations (or clear links) drive revenue.
- Photos of actual dishes outperform any copy; refresh seasonally.

## Compliance & credibility notes

- **Allergen and menu accuracy** matters, keep prices/items current; note "menu subject to change".
- Don't misrepresent sourcing ("organic", "local", "wild-caught") unless true.
- Alcohol/age-gated content where relevant (bars/wine).
- Structure/tone guidance only.
