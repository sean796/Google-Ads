# Dental & Orthodontics

Build a dental site the way someone who builds dental sites for a living would: calm, clean, credentials-forward, and engineered to convert an anxious first-time patient into a booked appointment.

## Brand voice & tone

- **Reassuring and pain-free.** Lead with comfort. Dental anxiety is the #1 reason people delay care, the copy's job is to lower it ("gentle", "judgment-free", "sedation options", "we'll explain every step").
- **Credentials-forward.** Doctor names with credentials (DDS/DMD), years in practice, board affiliations, technology ("digital X-rays, intraoral cameras").
- **Clean and clinical, not cold.** Whitespace, soft accent color, real photos of the actual office and team, never stock dentists.
- Avoid jargon; translate procedures ("root canal" → "we'll save the tooth, comfortably").

## Target keywords

Localize every term with `{city}` / `{neighborhood}`:

- Intent + place: `dentist {city}`, `emergency dentist {city}`, `family dentist near me`, `pediatric dentist {city}`
- Procedure: `Invisalign {city}`, `dental implants {city}`, `teeth whitening`, `same-day crowns`, `wisdom tooth extraction`
- Trust/insurance: `dentist that takes {insurer}`, `affordable dentist`, `new patient special`
- Long-tail: `does a root canal hurt`, `cost of Invisalign {city}`

## Page set & structure

Buyers expect: **Home · Services (or per-service pages) · New Patients · Meet the Team · Smile Gallery · Contact/Book.**

Homepage section order:
1. Hero, friendly team/office photo, one-line value prop, **Book / Call** + "New patients welcome"
2. Trust strip, insurance logos, "Accepting new patients", years/served count, review stars
3. Services grid, icon-box per service, linking to detail
4. Anxiety-reducer band, "Nervous about the dentist?" comfort/sedation copy
5. Meet the dentist, photo, credentials, short warm bio
6. Smile gallery / before-after (consent-permitting)
7. Reviews / testimonials
8. New-patient offer + booking CTA + map/hours

## Conversion patterns

- **Above the fold:** click-to-call (mobile) + "Request an appointment" form. Phone number visible in the header on every page.
- **Trust signals:** insurance accepted (logos), new-patient special, Google review count + stars, "X years serving {city}".
- **Booking flow:** short form (name, phone, preferred time, new/existing), don't ask for insurance details up front. Offer both online booking and a phone option.
- Sticky mobile call bar converts well for emergency searches.

## Compliance & credibility notes

- **No fake reviews / before-after without consent.** Patient photos need written release; never fabricate testimonials.
- Don't state guaranteed outcomes ("painless guaranteed"); use "designed to be comfortable".
- If collecting health info via forms, note privacy/HIPAA handling and keep forms minimal.
