# Therapy & Counseling

Covers talk therapy/counseling, massage/bodywork, chiropractic, and allied wellness practitioners. The throughline: a calm, safe, trustworthy space that lowers the barrier to reaching out.

## Brand voice & tone

- **Calm, safe, human.** Warm and unhurried; first-person, plain language. The visitor may be vulnerable, no clinical coldness, no hype.
- **Confidential and judgment-free.** Emphasize privacy, "a safe space", "at your pace".
- **Soft, grounded aesthetic.** Earthy or muted-cool palette, natural imagery, lots of breathing room.
- Pick the sub-voice: counseling = gentle/emotional; chiropractic & massage = relief/restoration ("feel better, move freely").

## Target keywords

- Service + place: `therapist {city}`, `counseling {city}`, `massage therapy {city}`, `chiropractor near me`, `couples counseling {city}`
- Need: `anxiety therapist`, `back pain relief`, `deep tissue massage`, `sports injury chiropractor`, `stress management`
- Access: `online therapy {state}`, `takes {insurer}`, `evening appointments`, `accepting new clients`

## Page set & structure

**Home · Services/Approach · About the practitioner · Pricing/Insurance · FAQ · Contact/Book.**

Homepage order:
1. Hero, calm imagery, empathetic one-liner, **Book / Reach out** CTA
2. "Is this you?" band, name the problem gently (stress, pain, overwhelm)
3. Services / modalities, what you offer and what to expect
4. About the practitioner, photo, credentials, warm personal "why"
5. How it works, first session expectations (reduces fear of the unknown)
6. Pricing / insurance / sliding scale
7. FAQ (accordion) + gentle CTA + location/telehealth note

## Recommended library combo

- **Brand Kit:** `holistic-earth` (default, earthy, restorative; ideal for massage/bodywork/holistic). Use `mindful-blue` for a calmer clinical counseling feel, or `calm-spa` for a soft, spa-like practice.
- **Prompts:** `massage-therapy` (primary for bodywork/massage/chiro) and `chiropractor` (clinical/relief angle). For talk-therapy practices, lean on the `massage-therapy` structure but rewrite copy to the counseling voice above.
- **Template:** none yet, build from structure.

## Conversion patterns

- **Low-friction first contact:** "Book a free 15-min consult" or "Send a message", vulnerable visitors won't fill long forms.
- **First-session expectations** section removes a major hesitation.
- Practitioner photo + personal "why" builds the trust that converts.
- Offer telehealth/online and evening options visibly, access is a deciding factor.

## Compliance & credibility notes

- **No outcome guarantees** ("cure anxiety", "fix your back permanently"). Use "support", "relief", "many clients find…".
- Mental-health and bodily-health info handled via forms needs a privacy/confidentiality note.
- State license/credentials and scope; don't imply medical claims beyond the practitioner's scope.
- Web-structure and tone guidance only, not medical, psychological, or legal advice; recommend a disclaimer and "consult a professional" note.
