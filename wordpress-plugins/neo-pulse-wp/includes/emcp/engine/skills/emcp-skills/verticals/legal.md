# Legal

Law firms and solo attorneys. A high-stakes, high-trust decision often made under stress (injury, divorce, charges). The site sells **authority, results, and approachability**, and gets the visitor to a consultation.

## Brand voice & tone

- **Authoritative and credible**: but human. Confidence without arrogance; clarity without jargon.
- Match the practice area's emotional register: personal injury = empathetic + fighter ("we fight for you"); estate/family = calm, caring; corporate = precise, polished.
- Conservative, professional aesthetic, deep navy/charcoal/burgundy, serif or strong sans, restrained. Credibility over flash.

## Target keywords

- Practice + place: `personal injury lawyer {city}`, `divorce attorney {city}`, `criminal defense lawyer {city}`, `estate planning attorney {city}`
- Intent/urgency: `free consultation lawyer {city}`, `{practice} attorney near me`, `best {practice} lawyer {city}`
- Situation: `what to do after a car accident {state}`, `how much does a divorce cost {state}`

## Page set & structure

**Home · Practice Areas (+ per-area pages) · Attorneys/About · Results/Case results · Reviews · Contact/Free consultation.**

Homepage order:
1. Hero, firm/attorney photo, value prop, **Free consultation** + phone (prominent)
2. Trust band, years practicing, cases won, settlements recovered, bar/awards (Super Lawyers, Avvo)
3. Practice areas grid, link to detail pages (SEO + clarity)
4. Why this firm, results, personal attention, no-fee-unless-we-win (if applicable)
5. Attorney bios, credentials, experience, approachable photos
6. Case results / testimonials (with disclaimers)
7. Consultation CTA + contact form + phone + office location

## Conversion patterns

- **"Free consultation" + click-to-call** are the dominant CTAs, legal leads are urgent and phone-heavy. Phone in the header on every page.
- **Results/settlements and credentials** (bar admissions, awards) carry the trust decision.
- Practice-area pages capture specific high-intent searches and convert far better than a generic homepage.
- Contingency messaging ("no fee unless we win") is powerful for injury practices, only if true.

## Compliance & credibility notes

- **Bar advertising rules are strict and vary by state.** Past results need a disclaimer ("prior results do not guarantee a similar outcome"); avoid "best", "specialist", or "expert" claims where the bar prohibits them.
- **No guaranteed outcomes.** Don't imply certainty of winning.
- Note that contacting the firm does not create an attorney-client relationship; include required disclaimers.
- This file is web-structure and tone guidance only, **not legal advice**; recommend the firm's own review of all advertising copy against its bar rules.
