# Med-Spa & Aesthetics

Aesthetic medicine sits between luxury beauty and clinical credibility. The site must feel aspirational and indulgent **and** signal that real medical professionals stand behind the needle.

## Brand voice & tone

- **Aspirational + clinically safe.** Glamour photography, but copy that reassures ("board-certified", "medically supervised", "consultation-first").
- **Results-led, never pushy.** Speak to confidence and self-care, not insecurity.
- **Editorial/luxe aesthetic.** Generous whitespace, refined serif/display headings, muted-but-warm palette, real treatment-room and result photos.

## Target keywords

- Treatment + place: `Botox {city}`, `lip filler {city}`, `laser hair removal {city}`, `microneedling near me`, `CoolSculpting {city}`
- Outcome: `wrinkle treatment`, `skin tightening`, `acne scar treatment`, `medical facial`
- Trust/offer: `med spa {city}`, `med spa consultation`, `first treatment special`, `membership pricing`

## Page set & structure

**Home · Treatments (menu + per-treatment pages) · About / Our Providers · Results (before/after) · Membership/Pricing · Book a Consult.**

Homepage order:
1. Hero, elegant imagery, value prop, **Book a consultation** CTA
2. Trust band, "medically supervised", provider credentials, certifications/brands (Allergan, etc.)
3. Treatment categories, injectables / skin / body, each linking out
4. Signature results, before/after carousel (consented)
5. Meet the providers, credentials + warm bios
6. Membership / financing teaser
7. Reviews + final consult CTA + location/hours

## Recommended library combo

- **Brand Kit:** `calm-spa` (default, clean, spa-calm). Use `fashion-luxe` for a high-end editorial aesthetic, or `boutique-hotel` for a luxe, indulgent hospitality feel.
- **Prompt:** `med-spa` (Health & Wellness).
- **Template:** none yet, build from structure.

## Conversion patterns

- **Consultation-first CTA** everywhere ("Book a free consult"), high-ticket treatments convert through a conversation, not a cart.
- **Before/after** is the strongest proof, feature it prominently (with consent).
- Membership / financing (e.g. monthly plans) lowers the perceived price barrier.
- Provider credentials near the booking CTA reduce safety anxiety.

## Compliance & credibility notes

- **Medical claims are regulated.** Avoid guaranteed results, "permanent", or disease-treatment claims. Use "results may vary".
- Before/after photos require **written patient consent**; never use stock as if it were a real result.
- Name the supervising medical professional, aesthetics is medical, not just beauty.
- Structure/copy guidance only, not medical or regulatory advice; advise a compliance review of treatment claims.
