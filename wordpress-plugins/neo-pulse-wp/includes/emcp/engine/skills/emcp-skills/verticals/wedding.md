# Wedding & Events

Venues, planners, florists, photographers, and event pros. Couples buy on **emotion and beauty first, logistics second**: the site must feel like the day they're dreaming of, then make inquiring effortless.

## Brand voice & tone

- **Romantic, elegant, aspirational.** Editorial wedding-magazine feel. Let stunning photography lead.
- **Warm and personal**: couples want to feel cared for, not processed. First-person, "we'd be honored to…".
- Refined type (elegant serif/script accents used sparingly), soft/airy palette, lots of whitespace. Avoid clutter, elegance is restraint.

## Target keywords

- Service + place: `wedding venue {city}`, `wedding planner {city}`, `wedding florist {city}`, `wedding photographer {region}`
- Style/scale: `outdoor wedding venue {city}`, `intimate wedding venue`, `barn wedding {region}`, `luxury wedding planner`
- Planning intent: `wedding packages {city}`, `how much does a {city} wedding cost`, `book a tour`

## Page set & structure

**Home · Gallery/Portfolio · Services/Packages · Venue/About · Pricing or "Investment" · Reviews · Inquire/Book a tour.**

Homepage order:
1. Hero, breathtaking single image, name, **Inquire / Book a tour** CTA
2. Intro line, the promise/feeling ("Where your forever begins")
3. Gallery preview, real weddings (the strongest selling tool)
4. Services / packages overview
5. Real-couple testimonials (emotional proof)
6. About / the space or the team
7. Inquiry form + CTA (capture the lead, bookings happen over conversation)

## Recommended library combo

- **Brand Kit:** `wedding-editorial` (default, elegant, magazine-style). Use `handcrafted-warm` for rustic/boho/artisanal venues and planners.
- **Prompts:** `wedding-planner` (planners/coordinators), `event-venue` (venues), `wedding-photographer` (photographers). Pick by sub-type; the structure is shared.
- **Template:** none yet, build from structure. (Backlog: a wedding/venue template, high-demand gap.)

## Conversion patterns

- **Inquiry form / "Book a tour"** is the goal, weddings are booked through conversation, not instant checkout. Keep the form short (date, guest count, name, email).
- **Galleries of real weddings** are the #1 converter, invest in them.
- Real-couple testimonials with photos build trust and emotion.
- "Investment starts at…" pricing transparency filters and qualifies leads (optional but appreciated).

## Compliance & credibility notes

- Use **real client photos with photographer credit and permission**.
- Be accurate on capacity, inclusions, and what packages do/don't cover, disputes here are costly.
- Don't promise availability/dates the site can't honor; route to a real inquiry.
- Structure/tone guidance only.
