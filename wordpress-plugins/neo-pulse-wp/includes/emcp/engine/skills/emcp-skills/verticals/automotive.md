# Automotive

Auto repair, service shops, detailing, dealerships, and driving schools. The buyer is choosing who to trust with an expensive machine, the site sells **honesty, competence, and convenience.**

## Brand voice & tone

- **Trustworthy and no-nonsense.** Honesty is the differentiator in a trade with a trust problem ("upfront pricing", "no surprises", "we'll show you what's wrong").
- **Competent and solid.** Bold, sturdy type; dark/industrial or clean-technical palette; real shop, bay, and team photos.
- Direct, confident copy, drivers want certainty, not flourish.

## Target keywords

- Service + place: `auto repair {city}`, `mechanic near me`, `brake repair {city}`, `oil change {city}`, `car detailing {city}`, `tire shop {city}`
- Trust/urgency: `honest mechanic {city}`, `same-day auto repair`, `check engine light {city}`, `free estimate`
- Make-specific: `{make} repair {city}` (e.g. `Toyota repair {city}`), `European auto specialist`

## Page set & structure

**Home · Services · Why Us / Reviews · Specials/Coupons · Book / Estimate · Contact.**

Homepage order:
1. Hero, shop/team photo, value prop, **Book service / Get an estimate** + phone
2. Trust band, years in business, ASE/manufacturer certifications, warranty offered, review stars
3. Services grid, repair categories with icons
4. Why us, upfront pricing, warranty, honest diagnostics
5. Reviews / testimonials (huge in this trade)
6. Coupons/specials, oil-change/inspection deals convert
7. Booking + hours + map (location matters, local searches)

## Recommended library combo

- **Brand Kit:** `mechanic-garage` (default, rugged/industrial) or `auto-garage` (clean garage). Use `tech-charcoal` for dealerships/EV/specialist shops wanting a modern technical look.
- **Prompts:** `auto-repair` (primary, repair/service shops) and `driving-school` (for driving academies).
- **Template:** `roadready-driving-academy`, **loose structural fit** (it's a driving academy, filed under Professional Services). Use it as a layout starting point for a driving-school build; for repair/detailing shops, build from the structure above. (Backlog: a true repair/detailing template.)

## Conversion patterns

- **Phone + "Get an estimate"** are the primary CTAs, many drivers call. Click-to-call in the header.
- **Reviews and certifications** carry the trust decision, feature both prominently.
- Coupons/specials and warranty offers tip the choice between nearby shops.
- Hours + location above the fold; "Open now" status helps urgent searches.

## Compliance & credibility notes

- Don't advertise prices/specials you won't honor; state expiry on coupons.
- Display real certifications (ASE, manufacturer) only if genuinely held.
- Warranty terms should be accurate; avoid absolute guarantees on repairs.
- Structure/tone guidance only.
